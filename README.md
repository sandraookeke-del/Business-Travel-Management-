# Business-Travel-Management-
This is a Business Travel Management Project focused on analysing travel data, travel expenses and generating recommendations and insights to the business travel patterns of employees to support data driven business decisions

# Project Objectives
- Analysing Business travel monthly activity
- Monitor and evaluate the number of travel
- Identify trends across travel categories and destinations
- Support data driven decision making for business travel planning

# Key Performance Indicators
- Total Travelers
- Total Trip Expenditure
- Average Age of Travelers
- Average Accomodation
- Average Trip Duration

# Tools
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot chat
- Power Querry
- Interative Dashboard

# Key Insights
- Travel Activity peaks between May and September
- The most frequently used means of transportation is by flight
- The average traveler age is 33years
- Hotel has the highest cost of accomodation

# Recommendations
- Tickets are more expensive during peak periods from May - September, they should be booked earlier to save cost
- Identify the cost effective transport systems after comparison to use it as a leverage to encourgae travelers to use without compromising the standard of transport
- Encourage travellers to use train means of transportation
- Encourage travellers to use Airbnb especially for those who love total freedom to themselves
- Travellers should be encouraged to travel in spring just before the peak t reduce ticket fares
